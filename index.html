How to Play

Every row and every column of the grid is a math equation.

  • Drag the numbers from the tray into the empty squares.
  • Work left to right — what you fill in must make every equation true.
  • Numbers are shared where rows and columns cross, so one wrong pick can affect two equations.
  • Tap a filled square to take a number back, or just drag it somewhere else.

Stuck? Use Hint to reveal one correct number, or Check to mark mistakes.
